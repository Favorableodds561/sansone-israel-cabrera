Myths vs. Reality
Common myths about bad credit — debunked.
1
The Myth
"You can't get approved with bad credit"
The Reality

Many lenders specialize in subprime financing. Israel works with multiple lenders who approve buyers with scores below 500 — especially with proof of income.

2
The Myth
"You'll need thousands down"
The Reality

Down payment requirements vary by lender and income. Israel structures deals around what you actually have available — not a fixed number.

3
The Myth
"Only beaters are available at your credit level"
The Reality

Israel gets bad-credit buyers into 2024–2025 model vehicles every week. Credit affects your rate, not the car you can drive.

4
The Myth
"Getting approved will hurt your credit more"
The Reality

On-time payments on an auto loan are one of the best ways to rebuild credit over 12–24 months. Israel explains how to use it strategically.

5
The Myth
"You've been turned down, so it won't work anywhere"
The Reality

Different dealers have different lender relationships. Being turned down elsewhere doesn't mean Israel can't find a path. He has options others don't.
