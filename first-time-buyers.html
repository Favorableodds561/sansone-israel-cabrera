Common Questions
First-Time Buyer FAQ
Do I need a co-signer if I have no credit? +
Not always. Israel works with lenders who have first-time buyer programs that consider employment and income, not just credit history. A co-signer can help but often isn't required.
How much do I need for a down payment? +
Manufacturer incentives sometimes allow $0 down. Typically Israel can work with $500–$2,000 to keep your monthly payment reasonable. He'll tell you exactly what's needed for your specific situation.
What's the difference between leasing and financing? +
Leasing means you pay for the vehicle's depreciation over 2–3 years and return it at the end. Financing means you own the car after paying it off. Israel breaks down both with real dollar figures for your situation.
Does Israel charge a fee? +
No. Israel's service is completely free to you as the buyer. He is compensated by the dealership — never out of your pocket. That's been his policy since day one.
